# Client disclosure (template)

> BrightWave uses **AdIntel**, an AI-assisted analytics system, to monitor your advertising
> performance every night, detect unusual changes, investigate likely causes and draft your weekly
> report.
>
> - **A named BrightWave account manager approves every recommendation.** AdIntel cannot change your
>   budgets, bids or campaigns on its own.
> - **Every number in your report comes directly from your ad-platform data** and is checked
>   automatically before the report is sent.
> - **Your data is never shared with other clients.** It is stored in BrightWave's environment,
>   separated by client in every query, and is never used to train AI models.
> - In this deployment, AI models run on infrastructure controlled by BrightWave (local models;
>   in production, Amazon Bedrock with data retention disabled and self-hosted models inside our VPC).
> - Every action — by a person or by the system — is recorded in a tamper-evident audit log.
> - Each report's footer names the system version and the person who approved any changes.
>
> Questions: your account manager or privacy@brightwave.example
