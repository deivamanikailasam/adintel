# When conversions drop to zero

## Rule
If conversions are exactly zero for two or more days while clicks and spend look normal, assume tracking is
broken, not performance. Stop all analysis and reporting for the brand and alert the account manager and the
technical lead the same day.

## Why
A report built on broken tracking tells the client their return collapsed and recommends pausing their best
campaigns. That is the most damaging thing the agency can send.

## Typical causes
A removed pixel or tag after a website release, a consent-banner change, a renamed conversion event, or an
expired API token on the conversions integration.
