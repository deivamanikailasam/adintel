# Budget reallocation after a budget increase

## Symptom
Campaign-level CPA rises sharply within days of a budget increase, while each ad set's own CPA is roughly
unchanged. Spend share has moved toward an ad set that was previously a small part of the campaign.

## Why it happens
When a campaign or ad-set budget is raised, Meta and other auction platforms often deliver the extra money
to the audience that is cheapest to reach, which is frequently a colder, less efficient audience. The blend
gets worse even though no individual ad set did.

## What to do
Move budget back from the ad set that gained share to the most efficient ad set, in steps of at most 25% of
the source ad set's daily budget per change. Re-check CPA after 3 to 5 days before making a second move.
Do not pause the expensive ad set outright: it still feeds the retargeting pool.

## What not to do
Do not raise the total budget further to "push through" the inefficiency. Do not change bids and budgets
on the same day; the effects cannot be separated afterwards.
