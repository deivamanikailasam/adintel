# BrightWave reporting guidelines

## Voice
Plain English, confident but never promising. Lead with what changed and why it matters for the client's
money. Use the client's own numbers; never estimate a figure that is not in the data.

## Recommendations
Every recommendation is described as a proposal that the account team will review with the client.
Mention the expected range of impact, not a single number, and say what evidence it is based on.

## Caveats
If any data was missing or analysis was limited, say so in a dedicated caveats section. Clients prefer an
honest gap to a polished guess.
