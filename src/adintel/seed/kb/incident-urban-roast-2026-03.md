# Incident: Urban Roast retargeting CPA inflation (March)

## What happened
After the retargeting campaign budget was raised for a spring promotion, Meta moved most of the extra
spend to AS-B Retargeting Cold. Campaign CPA rose by about half within four days while AS-A Retargeting Warm
kept its usual CPA.

## What fixed it
Moving $150 per day from AS-B Retargeting Cold back to AS-A Retargeting Warm brought campaign CPA down by
28% within a week. A second move was not needed.

## Lesson
Budget increases on mixed-temperature retargeting campaigns need an allocation check three days later.
