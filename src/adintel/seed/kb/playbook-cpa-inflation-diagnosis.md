# Diagnosing CPA inflation

## Checklist
Check, in order: tracking (are conversions recorded at all?), mix (did spend move between ad sets?),
auction pressure (did CPC rise across all ad sets?), audience fatigue (is frequency rising while CTR falls?),
creative decay (is CTR falling on the same creative for 2+ weeks?), and landing-page conversion rate.

## Interpreting the mix test
If more than half of the CPA change is explained by spend shares moving, the problem is allocation, not
performance. Fix allocation first; creative or audience changes would be treating the wrong cause.

## Interpreting auction pressure
If CPC rose 15% or more on every ad set at once, look for seasonal competition before changing anything.
In Q4 this is normal and usually should be accepted rather than fought.
